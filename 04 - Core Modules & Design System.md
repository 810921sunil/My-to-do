# 🎨 Core Modules & Design System

Back to [[00 - Life OS Vault Hub]]

**Life OS** features a modern glassmorphic dark mode UI built with React 18, Tailwind CSS, HSL gradients, dynamic micro-animations, and seamless local data persistence.

---

## 🧩 Comprehensive Feature Ecosystem (10 Advanced Modules)

### 1. 🎓 Academic & Student Ecosystem
- **Exam Prep & CGPA Predictor (`ExamCgpaCalculator.tsx`):** SGPA/CGPA target calculation engine, subject credit points weighting, and semester exam countdown timers.
- **College Hub (`College.tsx`):** Schedule timeline, class attendance tracker, and assignment deadlines.

### 2. 🔥 Consistency & Habit Tracking
- **365-Day Habit Heatmap (`HabitHeatmap.tsx`):** GitHub-style annual compliance grid, streak counters, and category routine matrix.
- **Digital Wellbeing (`DigitalWellbeing.tsx`):** Focus session timer, screen time tracking.

### 3. 📄 Career & Placement Engine
- **ATS Resume & Portfolio PDF Builder (`ResumeBuilder.tsx`):** 1-Click ATS-compliant printable resume generator with live editor.
- **AI Mock Technical Interviewer (`AiInterviewPrep.tsx`):** Interactive CS/DSA question simulator, sample answers, and mastery XP scoring.
- **Career Prep (`PlacementPrep.tsx`):** Mock technical tests and application pipeline.

### 4. 🎧 Audio Focus & Productivity
- **Focus Music & Binaural Beats Player (`FocusMusicPlayer.tsx`):** Procedural Web Audio soundscapes (Lo-Fi, Rain, 432Hz Binaural Beats) and Pomodoro timer.

### 5. 📦 System Maintenance & Governance
- **Data Backup & Restore Vault (`DataBackupManager.tsx`):** 1-Click full workspace JSON export/import, local storage statistics, and disaster recovery.
- **Weekly Executive Performance Audit (`WeeklyLifeReport.tsx`):** Sunday automated productivity audit report card (A+ to C grade), executive recommendations, and PDF exporter.
- **PWA Installation & Offline Engine (`manifest.json`, `sw.js`):** Standalone PWA installation support on Android/iOS/Desktop, theme color matching, and offline service worker caching.

### 6. 🥗 Health & Fitness Ecosystem
- **Nutrition & Macros Planner (`NutritionFitnessPlanner.tsx`):** 3-Litre water intake tracker (250ml glass grid), calorie/protein meters, and daily meal log.
- **Health & Workout (`HealthWellness.tsx`):** Workout routines and exercise logger.

### 7. 📋 Task & Project Lifecycle
- **Visual Project Kanban Board (`ProjectKanbanBoard.tsx`):** 3-Column visual board (To Do -> In Progress -> Completed) with 1-click status movement.
- **Task Manager (`TaskManager.tsx`):** Central task list, priority tags, and subtasks checklist.

### 10. 🎮 Gamification & Virality Engine
- **Gamified Life XP & Level System (`GamifiedLevelSystem.tsx`):** Level progression (Novice to Zen Titan), total XP calculator, unlocked achievement badges, and social media shareable cards.
- **AI Study Flashcards & Spaced Repetition (`AiFlashcardGenerator.tsx`):** 3D interactive flip cards, Anki-style spaced repetition interval buttons (Easy/Good/Hard), and subject deck presets.
- **Peer Study & Focus Room Hub (`StudyRoomHub.tsx`):** Virtual focus sprint rooms, room leaderboard, and collaborative Pomodoro timers.

### 9. 💰 Financial Analytics
- **Smart Finance Analytics (`SmartFinanceAnalytics.tsx`):** Category spending distribution bars, monthly savings targets, and transaction logs.
- **Finance Manager (`FinanceManager.tsx`):** Income/expense breakdown.
